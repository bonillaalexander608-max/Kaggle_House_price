# Historical experiments — not runnable entry points

These are the author's original exploratory scripts and R Markdown, preserved as research history. They depend on local paths, execution order and saved-session objects. Use the root pipeline for a fresh run.

The historical report includes unsupported statements about Nicaragua, normality implying coefficient bias, and strong causal/robustness claims. Its data are from Ames, Iowa. Those statements are not conclusions of the revised project. Some evaluations reuse observations used during fitting; training metrics must not be presented as out-of-sample performance.

The original HTML, logs, `.RData`, `.Rhistory`, and training prediction-interval workbook are not published. The workbook is not evidence of held-out interval coverage. Historical artifacts are not used as a source of verified leaderboard scores or revised model metrics.
